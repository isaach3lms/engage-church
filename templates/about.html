Who we are

A church for
Bloomfield

Engage Church is a new name for a congregation that has been part of Bloomfield for a long time. Same building on Christian Street, same commitment to this town, a renewed focus on helping people take a real next step with Jesus.

We believe the Gospel of Jesus Christ, revealed in Scripture, is the foundation of everything we do. Every week we gather to worship, grow, and encourage one another. We walk alongside people who are seeking Jesus, and we teach what He commanded.

Our mission does not stop at the doors. We are here to serve this community and share the hope of Christ well beyond our walls.
